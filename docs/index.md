# Welcome to the University of Glasgow TRE Handbook

The University of Glasgow Trusted Research Environment (UoG TRE) services enable researchers to analyse sensitive data in a secure environment. These services are operated by the University of Glasgow in partnership with data controllers such as the NHS who define governance and security suitable for accessing data for research purposes. These services are often described as 'Safe Haven's in Scotland or Secure Data Environments (SDE) in England.

The UoG TRE offers secure data hosting and remotely accessible analysis environments or 'workstations' that allow researchers to interact with sensitive data under the terms and conditions required by the data controllers. Sensitive data is often real-world data created when the public uses public services, such as the NHS. Legal provisions exist under the Data Protection Act and other statutes that permit the re-use of this data for research purposes.

Like all other TREs (or Safe Havens, or SDEs) in the UK, the UoG TRE service operates according to the Five Safes framework.

The UoG TRE uses a private cloud infrastructure at the University's external data centre. TRE Projects are isolated from one another, and strict user access controls who can access specific datasets. Compute resources can be allocated to workstations, including additional CPU cores, RAMs and vGPU.

## Roles

### TRE Operators

The TRE Operators are the staff team at the University of Glasgow who operate the service. We create the workspaces, user accounts and workstations.

### Project Principal Investigator

The named investigator who is the primary 'owner' of the TRE project workspace. Creates initial TRE application and raises requests for TRE User access and workstation customisations.

### TRE Users

Researchers who are allocated workstations attached to workspaces. Conduct analyses on the workstations and request file outputs from the TRE. Must be Safe Researchers (see Five Safes framework).

## Costings

The University of Glasgow TRE operates on a cost-recovery basis. Our latest Rate Card is available on request from [tre@glasgow.ac.uk](mailto:tre@glasgow.ac.uk).

For new projects, please contact [tre@glasgow.ac.uk](mailto:tre@glasgow.ac.uk) to discuss requirements and to obtain a formal costing for your grant award.
